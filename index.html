How to Request a Game Assignment

  • 1. Tap the game(s) you want — as many as you like.
  • 2. Add your name and email at the bottom, then Request Assignment(s) → Send Request. It comes straight to me; no email or text needed.
  • 3. Your request will be reviewed, and if the game is still available the assignment will be posted in Assignr.

⚠ A request isn't an assignment — games may already be covered. Your name and email are kept with the request so I can reply, and used for nothing else.
